# Help & Getting Started

FlockFront is still in beta. You may sometimes find something confusing or something that does not work as expected. This guide walks you through the basics.

## Getting Started

### 1. Finish setting up your store

1. From your seller dashboard, choose **Store Admin**.
2. Open each part of the page and fill in your store information, contact information, pickup details, and store policies.
3. Add clear pickup instructions so customers know what to expect. You may keep your exact pickup address private until after a customer orders.
4. Choose the kinds of products you plan to sell.
5. Select **Save** when you are finished.

Look over your information before you move on. You are responsible for keeping your store details, policies, prices, and availability correct.

[Open Store Admin]

### 2. Add your first item for sale

FlockFront calls the birds and products you sell "inventory."

1. Choose **Add Inventory** from your seller dashboard.
2. Choose what you want to sell: **Live Birds**, **Hatching Eggs**, **Poultry Products**, or **Equipment & Supplies**.
3. Fill in the information shown on the page. This may include the breed or product name, hatch date, available date, price, quantity, photos, description, and pickup details.
4. Check your work, especially the price and quantity.
5. If you are not ready to make the item visible, select **Save Draft**. You can finish it later from the **Saved drafts** section on the **Add Inventory** page.
6. When the item is ready for customers, select **Publish inventory**.

After publishing, the item will appear under **Inventory**. It will be visible to customers once your store is live and shown to customers.

[Add Inventory]

### 3. Preview your store

You can preview your store before customers can see it.

1. Choose **Store Admin**.
2. Select **Preview Store**. On a phone, the button may say **Preview**.
3. Check your store name, location, photos, pickup information, and policies.
4. Open each item and make sure its photos, price, quantity, and available date are correct.
5. Return to **Store Admin** if you need to make changes. Select **Save** after changing your store information, or **Save Changes** after changing an inventory item.

[Preview My Store]

### 4. Make your store visible to customers

Your store must be launched before customers can visit it.

1. Choose **Store Admin**, then open **Store status**.
2. Finish every required item shown on the page. You may need to finish billing and accept the Seller Terms.
3. Select **Launch Store**.
4. After the store is launched, select **Show Store**.
5. Select **Save** to make that change take effect.
6. Look for the message, "Storefront is live."

If **Launch Store** is not available, look for an unfinished item in **Store status**. Your store also needs at least one published item with quantity available.

[Open Store Admin]

### 5. Share your store or an item

Your FlockFront store is your own store. It is not placed in a shared marketplace. Customers find it through the links you share.

To share one item:

1. Choose **Inventory**.
2. Find the item you want to share.
3. Select **Share listing**.
4. Choose **Share now**, **Copy link**, **Share on Facebook**, or the email option.

To share your whole store, open **Store Admin**, copy the **Public store URL**, and paste it wherever you already talk with customers. You can share it on your website, Facebook page, social media, email, or in a text message.

[View My Store]

### 6. Keep your inventory up to date

Update an item as soon as its price, quantity, or availability changes.

1. Choose **Inventory**.
2. Find the item and open it.
3. Change the price, quantity, available date, photos, or description as needed.
4. Select **Save Changes**.

If an item is no longer available, remove it from sale or set its available quantity to zero so customers do not order something you cannot provide.

For live birds with age-based pricing, FlockFront uses the hatch date and the prices you entered to show the price that applies today. Check the listing from time to time to make sure the current price is right.

[Manage Inventory]

### 7. Handle a new order

1. Choose **Orders** from your seller dashboard.
2. Select the order you want to open.
3. Review the customer's name and contact information, the items and quantities ordered, the price, payment method, pickup choice, and any customer notes.
4. Contact the customer if you need to confirm pickup or make a change.
5. If the customer paid you, select **Mark paid**.
6. After the customer has received the order, select **Mark order fulfilled**.
7. If the order was paid at pickup, choose **Mark fulfilled and paid**. If payment is still due or was recorded another way, choose **Mark fulfilled only**.
8. After you are finished with the order, you may select **Archive order** to move it out of your active orders.

Do not mark an order fulfilled until the customer has received the birds or products.

[View Orders]

### 8. Manage your Breed Library

Your Breed Library keeps the breeds you raise and sell in one place. Adding them here makes them easier to choose when you add live birds or hatching eggs.

To add a breed from FlockFront's Breed Library:

1. Choose **Breeds** from your seller dashboard.
2. Select **Add Breed**.
3. Search for the breed you want to add.
4. Select the breed and add it to your Breed Catalog.
5. Add a clear description and photo so customers can learn about the breed.

If you cannot find the breed you need:

1. Select **Add Breed**.
2. Choose **Create Custom Breed**.
3. Enter the breed name and requested details.
4. Select **Create Breed** or **Create Custom Breed**, whichever is shown.

To update a breed you already use:

1. Choose **Breeds**.
2. Find and open the breed.
3. Update its photo, description, or other information.
4. Select **Save Changes**.

Keep breed descriptions simple and honest. Include the details customers are most likely to ask about, such as egg color, adult size, temperament, laying habits, or whether the breed handles hot or cold weather well.

[Manage Breeds]

## Common Questions

### Why can't customers see my store?

Check these items in order:

1. Choose **Store Admin** and open **Store status**.
2. Finish every required item shown there.
3. Make sure you have selected **Launch Store**.
4. Make sure the store setting says **Hide Store**. If the button says **Show Store**, select it and then select **Save**.
5. Choose **Inventory** and make sure at least one item is published and has quantity available.
6. Select **Preview Store** to make sure the store and item look right.

If customers still cannot see the store, contact FlockFront for help.

### How do I add a breed?

1. Choose **Breeds** from your seller dashboard.
2. Select **Add Breed**.
3. Choose a breed from the Breed Library. If the breed is not listed, choose **Create Custom Breed**.
4. Add the breed name and requested details.
5. Select **Create Breed** or **Create Custom Breed**, whichever is shown.

The breed will be added to your Breed Catalog. You can then choose it when adding live birds or hatching eggs.

### How do I change an item's quantity, price, or available date?

1. Choose **Inventory**.
2. Open the item you want to change.
3. Enter the new quantity, price, or available date.
4. Select **Save Changes**.
5. Open **Preview Store** if you want to check how the change looks to customers.

### How does age-based pricing work?

Age-based pricing lets the price of live birds change as they grow.

When you add or edit live birds, enter the hatch date and each price you want to use. FlockFront uses the birds' age to show today's price. If the hatch date or pricing is wrong, the price shown to customers may also be wrong.

Open the item under **Inventory** from time to time and make sure its current price is correct. Select **Save Changes** after making any correction.

### What does "Pay at pickup" mean?

**Pay at pickup** means the customer places the order through FlockFront but pays you when the order is picked up or delivered.

You decide which payment types you accept, such as cash or another method you have told the customer about. Do not ask the customer to send an advance deposit through an outside payment service for an order marked **Pay at pickup**.

After you receive payment, open the order and select **Mark paid**. If you are finishing the order at the same time, select **Mark order fulfilled**, then **Mark fulfilled and paid**.

### Where do I find new orders?

Choose **Orders** from your seller dashboard. Open an order to see the customer, items, pickup choice, payment information, and notes.

### What does the customer receive after ordering?

FlockFront sends the customer an order confirmation with the information entered with the order.

You are still responsible for filling the order and contacting the customer if the availability, price, or pickup details change. You can open the order and select **Resend order confirmation** if the customer needs another copy.

### How do I change my store information?

1. Choose **Store Admin**.
2. Open the part you want to change, such as store information, contact information, pickup details, or policies.
3. Make your changes.
4. Select **Save**.
5. Select **Preview Store** to check any large changes before sharing your store again.

### How do I hide my store for a while?

1. Choose **Store Admin** and open **Store status**.
2. Select **Hide Store**.
3. Select **Save**.

Customers will not be able to view the store or place orders while it is hidden. When you are ready to return, select **Show Store**, then **Save**.
